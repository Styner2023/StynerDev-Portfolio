Research Areas

Exploring the slave insurance market and how companies like Aetna, AIG, and New York Life profited from policies on enslaved people — revealing the financial architecture of American bondage.

Examining convict leasing and the contradictions of the Thirteenth Amendment — how the abolition of slavery gave rise to new forms of bondage through the American carceral state.

Integrating political science, economics, and history to understand how debt has shaped governance, sovereignty, and the lived experiences of communities across the African diaspora.

Investigating how forensic science and medical knowledge intersect with race, power, and the politics of identification in both historical and contemporary contexts.

Questions & Answers

Dr. Michael Ralph is Professor and Chair of Afro-American Studies at Howard University. His research integrates political science, economics, history, and medical anthropology through an explicit focus on debt, slavery, insurance, forensics, and incarceration. He holds a Ph.D. in Anthropology from the University of Chicago.

You can reach Dr. Ralph via email at Michael.Ralph@howard.edu or through the contact form on this website. For media inquiries related to Before 13th, please contact HarperCollins publicity.

Before 13th is a full-color graphic novel exploring the friendship and feud between Ida B. Wells and Frederick Douglass. It offers new insights into slavery and incarceration through the lens of the 1893 Chicago World's Fair and the history of convict leasing.
