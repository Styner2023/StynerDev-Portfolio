Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

A free, AI-powered education program for underserved communities of every age — from children in foster-care homes to elders in senior homes — across Nigeria, Suriname, and the United States. What Kuumba Code Is

A free path from consumer to creator

A student arrives using technology the way most of us do — passively, as a customer. They leave making things with it: content with a voice, an audience that grows, an income they control, and a platform they own outright. The transformation is the product.

Why I Support Them

I learned to code on a laptop I could not afford. She bought it.

Before there was any of this, there was a laptop I had no way to buy. She bought it. Every site I have built, every system I have designed, and every student I have taught since started on that machine. I did not have a way in. She made one.

She gave me somewhere to live. And she gave me back my father — after his long incarceration, it was her home and her table where we learned how to be family again.

She has never once kept a tab.

She does it without turning away from her own. Her husband and her children still have all of her; she simply keeps making the table bigger. And she does not look past what my father carried, what I carry, or what whole communities are still carrying.

She sees it clearly, and she stays.

Who This Reaches

Global or country-specific, restricted or unrestricted, cash or equipment — it all arrives as the same thing: a person with a laptop and a reason to open it.

Adebisi is one of them. She is why the numbers on this page are written as a year of operation and not as a number of seats — because the next person through the door should never be the one we ran out of room for.

The Ask

Two ways to fund this work

Some funders want their gift in a specific country. Others want it to go wherever it will do the most good. Both are welcome here — and every ask funds a full year of operation, not a fixed number of seats.

Unrestricted · Any amount

Global Founding Partner

Fund the mission, not the map. Your gift is not tied to one country — we direct it wherever the need is greatest that month: a cohort in Nigeria that outgrew its room, laptops that have to clear customs in Suriname, paid guest faculty in Oakland. Each country runs its own instructors, its own programs and its own costs, and those needs do not arrive on schedule.

Unrestricted support is the most useful money we can receive, because it lets us move the moment something is needed instead of waiting for the right funder to appear. A partner can fund the full international program, one country, one area of operation, or any combination of funding, technology, equipment, expertise and professional opportunities.

What it covers

  • Any country, any program
  • Equipment, instruction or connectivity
  • Whatever is most urgent that month
  • Reported back to you, line by line

What each ask actually funds

A country ask covers twelve months of operation end to end: a laptop and a year of data in the hands of every student we enrol, live online instruction, local facilitators on the ground, curriculum and project support, certification, and paid honoraria for the physicians, attorneys, professors, and technology executives who come and teach. We do not ask accomplished professionals to donate specialised work indefinitely, and we do not cap a country at a headcount — when a country is funded, we keep opening cohorts and we keep enrolling.

Nigeria — $900,000

Our largest and fastest-growing program, and the proof the model works. Funding here lets us keep opening cohorts as fast as students come to us, and puts equipment and connectivity in the hands of students who would otherwise share a device or go without one.

Suriname — $1,000,000

Our live ask, currently with national funders. This founds the program in the country where our founder was born — a full year of instruction, equipment, local coordination, guest faculty, and in-person visits, delivered with Surinamese partners on the ground.

United States — $400,000

Community-based and rooted at Canticle Farm in Oakland, open to whoever comes through the door. Lower overhead because the space and the community are already there — funding goes to equipment, instruction, and the faculty who teach.

Fund a country outright as its founding funder, or partner at whatever level fits. Full itemised budgets are available for each country. Talk to us.
