How Every Lesson Works

Every course on The Path runs the same way: live, virtual, 8 weeks, and free. Always. You do not need any tech background, and you do not need to be a writer. If you can send a message on your phone, you can do this. Each course runs as an 8-week quarterly cohort, and you work with the AI tools people are actually using right now.

What makes it work is how each lesson is built. Every single lesson follows the same four steps — Learn, See, Practice, Your Turn — so you are never dropped in the deep end. You watch the idea explained plainly, you see a real example, you try it with support, and then you make something that is yours. Your workbooks are teaching tools with completed samples inside, not blank pages you have to fill in alone. By the end, you have not just watched a course — you have actually made something.

Apply Now Apply Now
Step 1. Learn

The concept, explained plainly. We start with the idea itself — no jargon, no assumptions. Just a clear explanation of what you are about to do and why it matters.

Step 2. See

A worked example, done in front of you. Think of a creator posting "easy jollof rice, no blender" — simple, real, and something people actually want. You see exactly how it is made before you are ever asked to make your own.

Step 3. Practice

Guided, low-stakes reps. You try it yourself with support and completed samples to lean on. There is no pressure to get it perfect — this is where you build confidence one small step at a time.

Step 4. Your Turn

You apply it to your own project. This is where it becomes yours — real content, in your voice, that you made. By the end of Create, you walk away with a published voice and an AI content workflow you can keep using.
