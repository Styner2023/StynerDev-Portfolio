The Path: Our Courses

The Path is one journey in four courses — Create, Grow, Earn, and Build — taken in order. Each is free, live, and virtual, runs as an 8-week quarterly cohort, and ends in something you have built. The journey finishes when you launch your own website.

AI Music & Creative Technology

Where a song becomes a first lesson in AI

Kuumba Code teaches students and young creators — girls, boys, and rising artists across the United States, Nigeria, and Suriname — to use AI music tools like Suno. Music gives them an immediate way to feel the power of AI: they are not just watching technology happen, they are using it to create.

Create original songs

Turn an idea into a finished, original track with AI tools like Suno.

Write lyrics & stories

Tell their own stories through lyrics, turning culture and language into music.

Prompt with intention

Build better prompts and creative direction to shape the sound they hear.

Explore genre & structure

Understand melody, genre, and song structure like a real producer.

Use AI responsibly

Learn to use AI as an honest, responsible creative tool — not a shortcut.

Build confidence

Gain real confidence in technology by making something they are proud of.

Direct & produce

Practice creative direction and digital production from idea to release.

Earn in the creative economy

Connect music to content, media, and entrepreneurship in the creative economy.
