Our Students

Kuumba Code is built by and for underserved communities of every age — from children in foster-care homes to elders in senior homes — across Nigeria, Suriname, and the United States. They arrive with ideas and leave as creators and founders — building real projects, growing an audience, and earning on their own terms. This is who our community is.
