Questions Donors Ask Before They Give

Free program, real outcomes. Here is where your generosity goes, and why every gift is tax-deductible. Have another question? Email us at hello@kuumbacode.com.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. Kuumba Code is fiscally sponsored by Canticle Farm, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 46-1484633). Your donation is processed through the sponsor and is tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. You will receive a receipt for your records.

Do students ever pay to take part?

Never. The program is free for every student, always. There is no tuition and no hidden cost. That promise is only possible because of grants and donors like you who cover the full cost of a student's journey through The Path.

What does my gift actually pay for?

Your gift funds the four free courses that make up The Path — CREATE, GROW, EARN, and BUILD — along with the mentorship and tools each student needs. Every student leaves with something real they built: content, an audience, income strategies, and ultimately their own website and platform.

Can I sponsor one specific student?

Yes. Our Sponsor a Student level ($1,500) covers one student's full journey through all four courses. If you would like to fund a student or a whole cohort, email hello@kuumbacode.com and we will walk you through it.

Who are the students I'm supporting?

The underserved and less fortunate of every age — from children in foster-care homes to elders in senior homes — across Nigeria, Suriname, and the United States: talented, self-determined people who are too often left out of the tech economy. This is not charity for its own sake; it is an investment in people who are ready to build and simply need the door opened.

Can I give monthly instead of all at once?

Absolutely. A recurring monthly gift, at any level, gives us the steady footing to plan cohorts and keep the program free year-round. Let us know what works for you at hello@kuumbacode.com.

Can my company or foundation partner?

Yes. We welcome company sponsorships, foundation grants, and matching gifts. Our Founding Partner level funds a full cohort and helps the program grow across all three countries. Reach the founder directly at styner@kuumbacode.com.

How do I actually make a donation?

Use any Support button on this page or email hello@kuumbacode.com and we will send you everything you need. Gifts are made through our fiscal sponsor, Canticle Farm, so they are tax-deductible.
