What Kuumba Code Is

A free path from consumer to creator

Kuumba Code is a free, virtual AI-and-digital-skills program for girls and women. Over a four-course path — Create, Grow, Earn, Build — students learn to make content, grow an audience, earn income, and launch their own website or platform. It's taught live, in English and Dutch, and it's free for every student, always — funded by grants and donations. Every student leaves with something real that she built herself, not just theory.

The Name

Creativity, capability, community

Kuumba is the Swahili word for creativity — and the sixth principle of the Nguzo Saba: to leave your community more beautiful and beneficial than you inherited it. Code means both writing software and the code a person lives by. Together, Kuumba Code stands for creativity, capability, and community.

The Path

Four courses, taken in order

Each course runs live and virtual in 8-week quarterly cohorts, and each one is free. The website build is always the final capstone — by the end, you point to a platform you own.

COURSE 01

Create

Content & AI strategy. You leave with content you've made and an AI content workflow.

COURSE 02

Grow

Analytics, SEO & growth. You leave with an audience-growth system and SEO/analytics fluency.

COURSE 03

Earn

Lead generation, email & monetization. You leave with income strategies you can run.

COURSE 04

Build

Website, brand & platform. The capstone: you launch your own website or platform.

Your Turn

You apply it to your own project.

A running “easy jollof rice, no blender” creator example threads through the lessons, so every abstract idea lands on something concrete and familiar. Workbooks are real teaching tools with completed samples — never blank capture sheets.

Who It's For

Designed for complete beginners

  • Girls and women, roughly ages 16–55. Boys are welcome; everyone who shares the mission is welcome alongside them.
  • No prior coding or tech background needed. If you can use a phone, you can start.
  • Fully virtual, so it's location-flexible across the communities we serve.
  • Taught bilingually in English and Dutch, in small quarterly cohorts.

Who Builds It

Founded, led, and taught by a working developer

Kuumba Code is founded, led, and taught by Styner Stiner — a working full-stack developer and systems architect. It's incubated at Canticle Farm in Oakland, California, and fiscally sponsored by Canticle Farm, a 501(c)(3) — so donations are tax-deductible through the sponsor.

We don't rescue anyone. We hand people the keys. Students are creators and founders, and we speak to them that way.
