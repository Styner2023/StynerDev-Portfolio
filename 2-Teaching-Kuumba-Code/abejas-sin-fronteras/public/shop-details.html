About the bee. Scaptotrigona pectoralis is a native stingless bee of Central America — known to local beekeepers as a fierce defender of her hive. She works the wild flora of Nicaragua's tropical forests, gathering nectar from blossoms that no commercial honeybee will ever taste.

About the honey. Single-species, raw, unfiltered. The flavor is bright and slightly tart — if you enjoy a crisp Fuji apple, this honey will feel familiar in the best way. No blending, no heating, no additives. What's in the jar is what came out of the hive.

About the makers. Each jar is harvested by campesino families practicing meliponicultura — the traditional, low-impact stewardship of native stingless bees passed down across generations. Members of the Red Nicaragüense de Meliponicultura — Sureste.

Why is this honey different from store honey? Most supermarket honey is from European honeybees (Apis mellifera) and is typically blended, heated, and filtered. Ours comes from a single colony of native stingless bees, kept raw — so the flavor varies seasonally and the texture stays alive.

What does "agroecological" mean? It means the families who keep the bees also farm the surrounding land without synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers — protecting the same flora the bees forage on.

Will this crystallize? Yes, naturally — that's a sign it's raw. Place the jar in warm water for a few minutes to liquefy.
