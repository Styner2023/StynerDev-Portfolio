## Services offered

1. **Biochar production** — on-site biochar from forest biomass; soil amendment that locks carbon into the ground.
2. **Native plant nursery** — local pickup; online catalog (SKU count open).
3. **Defensible space** — fire-wise zone planning around homes and outbuildings.
4. **Fuels reduction** — brush clearance, ladder-fuel removal.
5. **Stream restoration** — bank stabilization, riparian planting, watershed work.
6. **Ponds** — design and construction.
7. **Bilingual trainings (EN/ES)** — workshops for crews, landowners, and partner organizations.

## Voice & tone

- **"We," not "I."** Crew-driven, not founder-only.
- **Specific over vague.** "From a single defensible-space day to a multi-year restoration plan" beats "we do everything."
- **Honest claims only.** Don't fabricate certifications, project counts, or testimonials. If a number is unknown, leave it out or use a verifiable one ("50+", not "200+").
- **Bilingual is core, not a footnote.** Surface EN/ES on the home page and CTAs, not just the trainings page.
- **Practical and earthy, never corporate.** Reference the watershed, the season, the land — not "synergies."
