## About Suriname

Suriname is a small country on the northeastern coast of South America. It's the smallest sovereign state in South America, with Paramaribo as its capital and largest city. Known for its diverse culture and tropical rainforests, Suriname has a unique automotive service landscape where mobile mechanics play a crucial role in vehicle maintenance.
